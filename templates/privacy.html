Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
